How to use it

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Tap the pages you want to rotate or use “Rotate all”.
  3. Click “Save rotated PDF”.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

Does rotating reduce quality?

No. Only the page orientation changes; the content isn't modified.
