Data controller

Your documents never leave your device

All Pagelo tools run inside your browser. The PDFs, Word documents and images you use are not sent to any server, not stored, and nobody else can see them, not even us. When you close the page, they're cleared from memory.

What data is processed

We don't require sign-up or collect your name, email or other personal data, unless you choose to write to our contact address. In that case we'll only use your message to reply to you.

Cookies and advertising

This website is free thanks to advertising from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and identifiers to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance and, if you agree, show you personalized ads based on your interests.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you'll be asked for consent before these cookies are used, through Google's consent platform, certified under the IAB TCF framework. If you don't agree, non-personalized ads may be shown.

Cookie settings aren't available right now (an ad blocker may be hiding them). You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

Other data stored in your browser

If you choose light or dark mode, that preference is saved in your browser (local storage). It can't be used to identify you.

When you pass a PDF from one tool to another with “What's next?”, the file is stored for a few minutes in your browser only and deleted as soon as it opens in the next tool. It never leaves your device.

Visitor statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to know how many people visit each page and from which country. It doesn't use cookies: each visit is identified by an anonymous code that is discarded after 24 hours, and only aggregated data is kept, which can't identify you or follow you across other websites.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data by writing to siegfriedwebia@gmail.com. If you believe we haven't handled your request properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
