Sign PDF

Draw your signature with your finger or mouse, type it or upload a photo, and place it anywhere on the document.

1. Create your signature

Sign on white paper with a dark pen and take a photo.

2. Place it

This signature is an image placed on the document, like signing a scanned paper. It's fine for most everyday paperwork. If you're asked for a qualified electronic signature (with a digital certificate), use the tool provided by the organization that requires it.

How to use it

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Create your signature: draw it, type it or upload a photo.
  3. Go to the page you want to sign and click “Place on this page”. Drag it and adjust its size.
  4. Add the date if you need it and click “Sign and download”.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

Is it legally valid?

A digitized handwritten signature is valid for many everyday documents (private contracts, authorizations, delivery notes). Some official procedures require an electronic signature with a digital certificate.

Can I sign several pages?

Yes. Change page with the viewer buttons and place your signature on each one.
