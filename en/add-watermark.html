How to use it

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Type the text or choose an image.
  3. Adjust the size, color, transparency and angle using the preview.
  4. Click “Add watermark”.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

Can the watermark be removed later?

It's embedded in the pages. Advanced editing software could erase it, but it's not something that happens by accident.

Can I use emojis?

They won't show up: standard PDF fonts don't include them. Use regular letters, numbers and symbols, or an image.
