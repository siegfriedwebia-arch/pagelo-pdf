    Frequently asked questions

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

    Which format should I choose?

    JPG for photos and maximum compatibility; PNG if you need a transparent background or sharp edges; WebP for websites, because it's smaller.
