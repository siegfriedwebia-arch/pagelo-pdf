How to use it

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Tap the pages you want to remove (they'll be crossed out) or type their numbers.
  3. Click “Delete pages” and download the result.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

Can I recover a deleted page?

Your original file isn't changed. If you make a mistake, just start again with it.
