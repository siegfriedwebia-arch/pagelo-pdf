Password protect a PDF

Add a password to your PDF so only people who know it can open it. 256-bit AES encryption.

Write the password down. If you forget it, there's no way to recover the PDF.

How to use it

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Type the password twice.
  3. Choose what can be done with the document and click “Protect PDF”.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

Is it secure?

Yes. It uses 256-bit AES encryption, the same standard as Adobe Acrobat. A long password is practically impossible to guess.

What if I forget the password?

It can't be recovered. Keep an unprotected copy of the original PDF too.
