    How to use it

    1. Choose your images.
    2. Enter the new width, height or percentage.
    3. Click “Resize images”.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

    What happens if I enter width and height with “Keep proportions”?

    The image is scaled to fit within those dimensions without distortion.

    Can I enlarge an image?

    Yes, up to 200%, but it won't gain detail: it will look a bit blurrier.
