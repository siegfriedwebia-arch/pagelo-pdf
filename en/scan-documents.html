How to use it

  1. Tap “Take photo or choose” and photograph the document on a dark surface.
  2. Drag the four corners to the edges of the paper.
  3. Choose an enhancement: “Document” removes shadows; “Black & white” is ideal for text.
  4. Add more pages if needed and tap “Create PDF”.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

How do I get the best photos?

Use good light and no flash, put the paper on a dark table and hold the phone parallel to it. Then adjust the corners carefully.

Do I need to install an app?

No. It works in your phone's browser, and your photos aren't uploaded anywhere.
