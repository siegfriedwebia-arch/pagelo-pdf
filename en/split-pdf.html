How to use it

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Choose how to split it: by ranges, every few pages, or one per file.
  3. Click “Split PDF”. If there are several files, they download together in a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

How do I write the ranges?

Separate them with commas and use hyphens for intervals. “1-3, 4-6, 7” creates three files: pages 1 to 3, 4 to 6, and page 7.
