  3. Click “Convert to PDF”.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

What if the table is very wide?

Automatic orientation switches to landscape when there are many columns, and the font size adjusts so they fit on the page.

Are charts copied?

No, only the cell data. To keep charts, export to PDF from Excel with File → Save As → PDF.
