How to use it

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Choose what to add: text, highlight, box, white-out or image.
  3. Drag each item into place; tap it to change its text, size or color.
  4. Click “Save PDF”.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

Can I change the PDF's original text?

PDFs weren't designed to be edited like a Word file. The most reliable way is to cover the text with white-out and type the new text on top, which is what most editors do.

Which font is used?

Helvetica, the standard PDF font, which looks the same on every device.
