DOCX → PDF

Word to PDF

Convert Word documents (.docx) to PDF with selectable text, headings, lists, tables and images.

Content preview

Text, headings, bold, lists, tables and images are kept. Headers, footers, columns and text boxes aren't copied. If you need the exact layout, in Word use File → Save As → PDF.

How to use it

  1. Upload your .docx document.
  2. Check the preview and choose the page size.
  3. Click “Convert to PDF” and download it.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

Do old .doc files work?

They can't be read in the browser. Open them in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice and save them as .docx.

Will it look the same as in Word?

The content will; the design may change slightly (fonts, headers and footers). For documents with complex layouts, save as PDF from Word.
