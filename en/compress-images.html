Compress images

Reduce the file size of your JPG, PNG and WebP photos. You can compress many at once.

Between 60% and 80%, the difference is barely noticeable.

Format

PNGs only get smaller when you reduce their dimensions. To save more, choose JPG or WebP.

Choose one or more images.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your images.
    2. Adjust the quality and maximum size.
    3. Click “Compress images”.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

    How much smaller do they get?

    Phone photos usually end up 60% to 90% smaller when limited to 1920 px at 75% quality.
