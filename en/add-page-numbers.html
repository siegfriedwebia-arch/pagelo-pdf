Add page numbers to a PDF

Add the page number in a corner or the center, in the format you prefer.

To skip the cover page, start on page 2.

How to use it

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Choose the position, format and which page to start numbering from.
  3. Check the preview and click “Add page numbers”.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

Can I skip the cover page?

Yes. Set “Start numbering on page” to 2. If you want that page to show 1, leave “First number” at 1.
