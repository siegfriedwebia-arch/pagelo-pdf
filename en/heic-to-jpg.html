    Frequently asked questions

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

    Why are my iPhone photos HEIC?

    iPhones save photos as HEIC because they take up half the space of a JPG. But many programs and websites don't accept it.

    How do I make my iPhone take photos in JPG?

    In Settings → Camera → Formats, choose “Most Compatible”. New photos will be saved as JPG.
