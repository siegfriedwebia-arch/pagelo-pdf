Organize PDF

Change the page order, rotate pages, remove the ones you don't need or add pages from other PDFs.

Drag with ⠿ or use the ‹ › arrows to move each page.

How to use it

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Move pages by dragging the ⠿ handle or with the arrows.
  3. Rotate (↻) or delete (✕) any pages and click “Save PDF”.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

Can I combine pages from several PDFs?

Yes. Click “Add another PDF” and its pages will appear at the end; then move them wherever you want.
