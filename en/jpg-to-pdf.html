    How to use it

    1. Choose your images.
    2. Put them in order by dragging them or using the arrows.
    3. Choose the size, orientation and margin, and click “Create PDF”.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Everything happens in your browser: your documents never leave your device and nobody else can see them.

    Do the photos lose quality?

    No. JPGs and PNGs are inserted as they are, without recompressing them. If the PDF is too big, run it through “Compress PDF” afterwards.

    Can I use iPhone photos (HEIC)?

    Yes. HEIC photos are converted automatically before they're added to the PDF.
